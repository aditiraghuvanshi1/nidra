    Buying guide

    How to choose a mattress

    Importance of a good mattress for sleep

    You spend about a third of your life on it. A mattress that sags or sleeps hot breaks your sleep into pieces, and you pay for it the next day in focus, mood and back pain.

    Good support keeps the spine in its natural curve. Good pressure relief keeps shoulders and hips from pushing back at you at 3 a.m.

    Things to consider while buying a mattress online

    Start with your sleeping position: side sleepers usually want softer, back and stomach sleepers firmer. Then check the trial period, the warranty terms, and whether returns are free.

    Look at the material as well. Foam is quiet and contouring, spring is bouncy and airy, latex is springy and durable. Read recent reviews from people of your build.

    How to decide the right mattress size

    Leave about 15 cm beyond your height, and give each sleeper at least 70 cm of width. Measure the room, not just the bed frame.
